RooTreasury is a personal finance management app for SailfishOS. It lets you track income and expenses month by month, showing the monthly balance and total available money. Expenses are organized into categories (Bills, Mortgage, Groceries, Health, Fuel, etc.) and displayed through a pie chart with their respective percentages.
